# LA2 prepared assets

`docs/la2-copy.json` is the authoritative eight-locale copy and sample source.
`scripts/generate-la2-copy.py` produces both platforms' resources and exact-locale
sample maps without reformatting unrelated iOS strings.

The sample is original illustrative text, not a quotation, hadith, translation of
a hadith, authenticity claim or ruling. Its source is Hadithly and it is dedicated
under CC0 1.0. This avoids assigning unverified rights or authority to religious
content in an onboarding demonstration. The label and caution stay visible.

The book artwork was made with the built-in ImageGen tool from the owner's
approved storyboard. Both native bundles contain the same `book.png` bytes. The
original remains in the user's generated-images library. No text is part of the
image; the native UI draws accessible localized labels.
