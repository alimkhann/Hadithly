## Decisions to carry into C and D

These are product judgments from the evidence below, not measured Hadithly results.

1. Prioritize a readable hadith in the chosen language and a clear next action.
   Match the promise across the website, onboarding and paywall. Show what readers
   can do, with actual product views and honest availability. UserOnboard's writing
   and the Zumba practitioner's account support this direction. Neither proves an
   uplift for Hadithly.
2. Use short, coherent screens for necessary choices. Keep optional education at
   the point of use. A single long form and a long sequence of unnecessary screens
   can both fail. Inspect language selection, auth cancellation and the first
   reader hint on both platforms. Do not add an onboarding survey just because a
   fitness app used one.
3. Make translation status understandable before decorating the reveal. Distinguish
   unavailable text, a retryable failure and an exhausted allowance. Explain the
   useful next action in place. Use motion to explain the state change, preserve
   reading position and allow interruption. Research here supports clarity and
   timely feedback; it does not prescribe a spring curve or duration.
4. Keep returning easy and private. Resume reading without stacked promotions.
   Let a short reading end without a completion demand. Reject streak loss, guilt,
   wagers, points, rankings and forced daily goals. Existing optional reminders
   remain a reader choice; this research authorizes no new messaging or tracking.
5. Keep the owner's monthly-equivalent paywall prototype distinct from release
   approval. Explain the benefit and allow a visible exit. Zumba's more frequent
   paywalls and Superwall's cross-app correlations do not justify obstructing free
   reading or changing prices/entitlements. The actual annual-total release gate
   remains open.
6. Separate first useful reading, voluntary return and paid renewal in later A7
   measurement. Duolingo's own model makes their different denominators explicit.
   Existing clipped totals cannot answer any cohort-return question. Verify C/D
   with rendered journeys now; do not introduce a measurement system during B.
