## 24. Built With Science: quiz traffic

[EyoYQU6JyV8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyoYQU6JyV8), Sub Club, 59:38.

- [17:11–20:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyoYQU6JyV8&t=1031s): Ethan Ethier says they spent a year improving the product before scaling ads, and distinguishes warmer organic visitors from paid visitors. Apply attention to useful reading and reliable return before acquisition spend.
- [25:01–27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyoYQU6JyV8&t=1501s): their quiz both explains the product and supplies workout-plan inputs. Web reportedly converts better than iOS; Android reportedly matches web. Reject a universal claim that a longer web quiz improves conversion. Hadithly needs useful language/reading choices, not a fitness intake questionnaire.
- [37:40–42:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyoYQU6JyV8&t=2260s): identify the problem before proposing variants, document the hypothesis, and reuse useful messaging. Apply this to the actual C/D screen defects.
- [53:00–57:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyoYQU6JyV8&t=3180s): annual-first trial presentation reportedly changed plan mix; more explanatory copy hurt another test. Reject hiding plan choices or framing a legitimate alternative as a loss.

Evidence quality: operator self-report without assignments, sample sizes or
uncertainty. The selected 25:30 and 54:05 frames show the speaker, not the quiz,
paywall or experiment data. Those designs therefore remain verbal descriptions.

## 25. Coconote: utility, reliability and onboarding

[Wg70HlO-5gY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wg70HlO-5gY), Sub Club, 65:28.

- [12:00–16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wg70HlO-5gY&t=720s): losing a recording exposes how much trust the product holds. Customer descriptions supplied their store headline. Apply reliable Saved/notes/position behavior and concrete reading benefits; reject presenting polish as proof of reliability.
- [27:00–37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wg70HlO-5gY&t=1620s): novelty videos drew views without payment, while problem-solving content performed better. Paid promotion also failed to meet their profitability target. Reject equating virality with demand or paid retention.
- [47:45–50:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wg70HlO-5gY&t=2865s): founders attribute a 16% trial-start increase to longer onboarding and describe moving account creation later. Apply a coherent optional sign-in journey. Reject importing their 15 screens or rearranging purchase identity handling without Hadithly-specific evidence.
- [50:45–52:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wg70HlO-5gY&t=3045s): web cancellation saves reportedly favored trial extensions over discounts. No Hadithly cancellation intervention follows; native/store behavior was not established by that web result.

Evidence quality: founder retrospective and commercial podcast; financial and
conversion claims lack independent cohort evidence. All three sampled frames show
speakers, not the onboarding or cancellation interface.

## 26. Phil Carter: first value and feature noise

[UYIgu02h8cs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYIgu02h8cs), Sub Club, 66:54.

- [06:15–12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYIgu02h8cs&t=375s): a first session should deliver something useful. Apply reading in the selected language and a restrained translation reveal. Reject personality quizzes, badges or unapproved paid generation merely to create spectacle.
- [28:00–30:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYIgu02h8cs&t=1680s): more features can reduce usable value by adding noise. Apply a smaller reader settings presentation, distinct controls and contextual help. New features are not the default research outcome.
- [46:00–51:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYIgu02h8cs&t=2760s): the guest and host debate cost, response speed and output quality. Apply clear waiting/allowance states. Reject substituting a cheaper model without semantic evidence or changing the owner's GPT-only/provider boundaries.
- [61:00–64:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYIgu02h8cs&t=3660s): different clients reportedly saw a 75% LTV increase and a greater-than-50% conversion decline after freemium changes. Neither identifies a universal winning access model.

Evidence quality: advisor examples and opinion, including unnamed clients and
combined interventions. Chosen frames show Phil Carter speaking. No experiment
dashboard or product demonstration substantiates the quantitative claims.

## 27. Zumba: guided choice and complete funnel outcomes

[-GHpDJm8MgY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-GHpDJm8MgY), Sub Club, 47:41.

- [10:00–16:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-GHpDJm8MgY&t=600s): research included beginners and people who churned, and guided programs reportedly improved use and retention. Apply a clear starting/resume option. Enrolling in a program and remaining active are associated here; doubling retention is not an isolated causal result.
- [25:00–33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-GHpDJm8MgY&t=1500s): web checkout initially lost conversions while the team reports a 17% LTV gain overall. Apply the habit of checking downstream outcomes, not the checkout migration. No billing, fee or policy decision is authorized.
- [38:00–42:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-GHpDJm8MgY&t=2280s): longer onboarding supplies actual recommendation inputs; one beginner class remains accessible without a trial. Apply useful choices followed by content. Reject collecting answers that do not affect the experience.
- [43:00–46:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-GHpDJm8MgY&t=2580s): three classes predict later use, while pricing, content and funnel changes all contribute to reported improvement. Reject importing a three-reading target, badges or daily pressure.

Evidence quality: direct operator account, with explicit multiple changes and no
raw experiment evidence. The 15:50, 26:10 and 38:35 frames are interview views,
not the program screens or LTV calculation.

## 29. Superwall: monetization consultancy examples

[24QMRDXck70](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70), Superwall, 43:03.
The title is "Our No-Code Apps Made $50M Using THIS Paywall"; the actual interview
is about the guest's work with client apps. The title does not establish audited
revenue or a no-code construction method.

- [04:00–07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=240s): copying another app's paywall can fail; an unnamed client's install-to-paid improvement is reported without data. Apply context, reject the offered conversion benchmarks as Hadithly targets.
- [10:00–14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=600s): trial timelines explain later billing, but the speaker also recommends emphasizing "free" repeatedly and reducing duration emphasis. Apply clear trial expectations only; reject making a subscription resemble a free product.
- [27:00–30:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1620s): the 28:15 frame shows a separate plan sheet with a sale and "9 spots remaining". Reject artificial scarcity and hiding prices behind another step to increase commitment.
- [33:00–42:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1980s): renaming a plan "Free", abandonment offers and session-start paywalls are advocated. Reject this pressure sequence for Hadithly's quiet reading journey.

Evidence quality: actual client-design walkthrough plus consultancy/sponsor
promotion. Screens prove the proposed presentation exists, not its causal effect,
truthful scarcity or fit for Hadithly.

## 30. Clear30: expectations and early rating pressure

[cYcU4_HQdIk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk), Superwall, 32:14.
The app discussed is Clear30 for cannabis use; the broad smoking title is not a
reason to interpret it as a tobacco study.

- [09:00–15:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=540s): founders explain their goal/pain questions and credibility presentation. Apply concrete benefits and honest source access. Reject shame, comparative personal deficits or guaranteed spiritual/health outcomes.
- [17:20–19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1040s): they state explicitly that the product costs money and report less conversion anxiety. Apply clear Free/Pro expectations; the stated small lift has no sample or uncertainty.
- [21:00–22:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1260s): the 21:25 frame shows the native rating prompt before the core experience, beside favorable reviews. Reject soliciting ratings before a meaningful reading or presenting rating volume as retention evidence.
- [24:35–26:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1475s): replacing feature slides with a concrete program timeline reportedly changed paywall conversion from about 20% to 30%. Frames 25:25/25:45 show that timeline and visible yearly billing copy. Apply concrete expectations, not the clinical claims or a seven-day reading program.

Evidence quality: founder self-report and observed Figma/prompt views. No raw
randomized results, clinical efficacy or Hadithly uplift are established.
