## Decisions to carry into C and D

1. Make the first useful reading and its language availability obvious. Keep one
   primary next action, clear native auth/cancel/recovery paths and existing
   guest entry. Restore the actual reading position on return.
2. Use motion to explain translation and focus-state changes. Keep it brief,
   interruptible and accessible. Preserve controls and text comprehension.
3. Inspect current real data on both platforms before cutting or decorating
   controls. Preserve relevance order, accurate state and useful guidance.
4. Use D's approved artwork and authentic app renders consistently. Describe
   current capabilities without fabricated uplift, universal accuracy claims,
   unverified testimonials or invented reader numbers.
5. Reject streaks, voting, guilt, extra commitment questions and long onboarding
   as a conversion tactic. Keep A3 source/semantic proof and A7 cohort evidence
   open; these videos provide hypotheses and observed examples, not Hadithly
   retention, financial, licensing or release evidence.
