## Claims that must not become product promises

The diaries correct earlier retention figures and change denominators between
trial starters, signups and downloads. Video 20's displayed funnel does not
validate its narrated signup rate. Video 19 describes a judge accepting invented
citations on a small difficult-case set. These are reasons to retain exact-source
and semantic checks, not a reason to add impressive citation counts.

Long onboarding, hard paywalls, quizzes and more screens are context-dependent
tactics. The Superwall comparison combines trials and purchases and excludes
zero-transaction paywalls. Zumba changes acquisition, copy, pricing and paywall
timing together. Neither isolates the effect of onboarding length. Do not copy
their reported numbers, religious guilt, streaks, votes, countdown pressure,
artificial processing delays or public social proof.

The reviews justify bounded C/D design decisions. They establish no Hadithly
cohort baseline, causal uplift, native-speaker approval, current store policy,
content rights, successful purchase or release readiness. A4–A7 resume after D.
