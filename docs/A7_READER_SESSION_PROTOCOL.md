# Run the proposed consented reader study

Draft protocol, October 31, 2026. Status: NOT STARTED. All templates below are
blank. This unit authorizes no contact, recruitment, observation, recording,
account action, purchase, analytics activation or external message.
Read the [measurement definitions](A7_MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) before later execution.
Keep the [41 carried gates](A7_GATE_CARRY_FORWARD.md) open.

## Prepare one bounded study

Use one 21-day batch with six to eight adult volunteers, aiming for at least
three iOS and three Android readers. Set a hard maximum of eight. This is a
qualitative convenience sample, not a representative retention experiment.
Use the participant's familiar language and explicitly selected physical phone.
Do not use the phone involved in the unresolved G37 incident without its separate
owner-context and device approval. Avoid collecting age, location or demographic
details. Record only adult eligibility confirmed, platform, broad OS/build,
task language, accessibility preference if voluntarily needed, and study code.

Include new-to-Hadithly readers for the core guest-first task. Invite variation
in reading familiarity and, when approved and supported, RTL or larger-text use.
Do not treat religious knowledge or religious identity as an eligibility field.
Offer plain instructions and assistance without judging the reader. Keep any
existing-account journey optional and separately approved. Never require account
creation, credentials, payment, generation, upload, private notes or push permission.
Do not infer speaker approval or full accessibility coverage from participation.

The supported UI locales remain en, ar, ru, kk, ky, uz, tr, id, ur, fr, es-419,
pt-BR, sw, hi and bn. A1's recorded actual first-reading text covers only
en/ar/ru/kk. Even these need current rights, safety and usable exact-text checks
before a real session. Do not offer unavailable locales as working content or
present a known meaning reversal. A participant choosing an unavailable language
may test availability messaging only with separate clear consent; exclude that
lane from delivered-first-reading evidence. No silent fallback to another language.

## Use this consent wording

Fill the brackets privately after owner review. Have a qualified speaker review
the exact version for each used language. Do not promise legal sufficiency or
publish this draft as a policy.

> We are testing whether Hadithly makes reading and finding your place clear.
> We are testing the app, not you. Taking part is your choice. You can skip any
> task or stop at any time without losing access to Hadithly.
>
> Today takes up to 30 minutes. If you choose follow-up, you may keep a short
> private diary for two weeks and have one conversation lasting up to 15 minutes
> after day 15. You do not need to return to the app. No return is a useful answer.
> We will not send reminders to read.
>
> [Named facilitator] will watch only the agreed Hadithly tasks. We will write
> brief notes using a study code about whether text appeared, where controls were
> unclear, help needed, and your optional diary answers. We will not record audio,
> video or your screen. Do not show passwords, notifications, personal notes,
> financial information or other apps. We do not ask about your beliefs or copy
> what you read. You may hide the screen or ask us to pause at any time.
>
> The study notes are private research records. They will not be linked to an
> app account or sent to analytics, AI tools or the public. [Named facilitator]
> can access them in [approved encrypted local location]. Contact details for the
> optional follow-up stay separate from task notes. This study does not require
> signing up, buying anything, uploading a file or enabling analytics. Ordinary
> network reading may still use Hadithly's existing service requests under its
> disclosed policy; if that is not acceptable, we will not start.
>
> Contact [approved withdrawal route] with your study code or withdrawal token
> to stop follow-up or remove your study notes until [day-28 calendar date]. We
> will confirm deletion within seven days and by that deadline. Contact details
> will be removed after follow-up closes on [day-21 calendar date]. Coded notes
> will be deleted on [day-28 calendar date]. Only non-identifying combined
> findings will remain after that date. We cannot remove an individual response
> from those combined findings once the code link is destroyed. No identifiable
> quote or participant breakdown will be published.
>
> Product analytics is separate. The study will proceed only with collection off.
> If you previously sent anonymous totals, we cannot identify or subtract your
> part from those totals. Leaving this study does not delete your app account or
> cancel a subscription. We can explain those separate controls without using
> them for you.
>
> Do you understand the notes, follow-up choices and deletion deadline, and do
> you freely agree to the observed session? May we separately contact you once
> after day 15 for follow-up? You can say yes to the session and no to follow-up.

Record each choice separately. Do not preselect consent or interpret silence as
agreement. If a participant cannot explain what will be observed and how to stop,
clarify once in familiar wording. If understanding remains unclear, do not enroll.
There is no recruitment or incentive budget approved. If compensation is later
proposed, approve and disclose the exact amount first, independent of return,
purchase, task success or withdrawal.

## Follow the facilitator script

1. Before the participant arrives, complete the run card. Replace every bracket
   in the consent wording and set actual calendar dates. Keep analytics off.
2. Say, "We are testing the app. Please do what makes sense to you. You can stop,
   skip a task or ask me to look away." Ask the participant to hide notifications.
3. Explain the consent in their language. Record session and follow-up choices
   separately. Give the participant their code/token and withdrawal instructions.
4. Ask, "Without making an account, find something you can read in your chosen
   language." Start the task timer. Watch the first visible usable text, not just
   a screen opening. Allow silence before helping. Cap this task at ten minutes.
5. Ask, "Where would you look to understand the source or translation notice?"
   Record discoverability and their paraphrase category, not beliefs or content.
6. Ask, "Save a place you might want to find again. Use a bookmark or favorite.
   Please do not write a personal note." Ask them to return to that place.
7. Offer, "If text size or direction is uncomfortable, adjust it as you normally
   would." Observe only volunteered needs. Do not change device accessibility
   settings without consent, and restore any agreed temporary change.
8. If the participant agrees, show the approved nontransactional offer task.
   Ask, "What would be charged and how often?" Stop before any purchase action.
   If the actual annual total is unavailable, mark the task blocked. Interest is
   optional and never counted as a purchase.
9. Say, "You do not need to open the app again for us. If you happen to use it,
   you may keep the small diary. You can also mark no use or unknown. Please do
   not write what you read." Explain the return windows without encouraging use.
10. Close within 30 minutes. Confirm withdrawal details and optional follow-up.
    Do not ask for a rating, testimonial, streak, referral or purchase.
11. After the last return window, and only with contact permission, send the one
    separately authorized follow-up invitation. Ask first about voluntary diary
    use. Only then offer the arranged resume task, labelled prompted.
12. Close all follow-up by study day 21. Reconcile missing and withdrawn records.
    Report counts, evidence limits and blockers. Delete according to the agreed
    dates; do not extend observation to chase an attractive result.

## Use a fixed three-week schedule

Set calendar dates only after start approval. Enroll the batch on days 0-2, so
all day-14 windows finish by day 17. Use each participant's first-render hour
for return windows, not a shared midnight or onboarding start.

| Study day | Allowed later activity after separate authorization | Evidence and stop boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before day 0 | Resolve run-card fields, approve exact invitation and contact audience; no reader contact while approval is pending | Start only with owner approval, understood consent, safe content/device and private storage. Otherwise remain NOT STARTED |
| Days 0-2 | Up to eight agreed baseline sessions, at most 30 minutes each | Witness rendered activation, assistance and blockers. Declined follow-up joins baseline evidence only |
| Each participant's days 1, 7 and 14 | Participant may keep the minimal diary voluntarily | No agent/operator monitoring, nudges, analytics activation or background app observation |
| Days 15-17 | One approved contact per consenting participant after their final window; optional 15-minute conversation and prompted resume | Collect diary or recall status, mark missing/unknown. Scheduled resume cannot prove voluntary return. No chasing nonresponders |
| Days 18-21 | Reconcile aggregate findings and owner acceptance review | Separate observed/reported/unknown, maturity, scope and payment unavailability. Close study at day 21 even if evidence is incomplete |
| By day 21 | Delete follow-up contact map after final contact or withdrawal | Record deletion confirmation without contact details |
| Day 28 at latest | Finish withdrawal requests, recompute combined findings, destroy coded notes and links | Retain only non-identifying issue/count summary and deletion receipt. No automatic extension or cloud backup |

The observation period is three weeks. Day 28 is the deletion deadline, not a
fourth week of extra observation. If approval delays the start, move the entire
batch before enrollment. If a participant starts late, do not shorten a return
window or silently extend the approved end date. Record that window unavailable.

## Stop, withdraw and delete

Stop immediately if consent is withdrawn, private material appears, a task could
spend money or expose credentials, unauthorized telemetry leaves the device, or
content/rights/safety cannot be established. A crash, lost reading place or blocked
locale ends that task and becomes a dated issue. Do not improvise a cloud fix,
account operation, paid generation, upload or physical-device recovery during a
session. Keep unrelated data untouched.

Only the named facilitator accesses the encrypted notes. Keep any follow-up
contact map separate, with code, contact permission, one chosen contact route and
expiry. Keep no code-to-account, device-ID or transaction mapping. Do not copy
study data into AI tools, issue trackers, screenshots or synced folders. Do not
back it up to another location without a new approved retention/deletion design.
Avoid exact quotes. Do not publish participant-level records or small subgroup
breakdowns; generalize a unique issue so a participant cannot be recognized.
