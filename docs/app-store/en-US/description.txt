Hadithly is a quiet place to read the seven major hadith collections in Arabic and in your preferred translation language.

READ WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Open the library and begin reading as a guest. An account is optional and adds private sync across your devices.

A READER THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
Move through content-sized pages, open a collection's volume list, adjust Arabic type size, and return to the hadith where you stopped.

SAVE WHAT MATTERS
Keep private bookmarks, favorites, notes, and reading progress. Signed-in readers can carry them across devices.

DAILY HADITH
Return to a daily selection or choose an optional notification time. Tapping the daily card opens its exact place in the reader.

TRANSLATIONS WITH CONTEXT
English source translations are shown when available. For supported languages, signed-in readers can request a limited number of AI translations. AI text is clearly labeled and includes available grounding sources; it is never presented as authoritative.

COMMUNITY CORRECTIONS
Suggest a translation from the hadith itself. Automated review checks the proposal before it enters the private review process. There are no votes, leaderboards, public scores, or streaks.

Hadithly Pro adds more AI translation requests. The paywall appears only after the free AI allowance is used. Reading, saved items, and existing translations remain available without a subscription.

Hadith translations and authenticity information can contain scholarly differences. Consult qualified scholars and primary sources for religious guidance.
