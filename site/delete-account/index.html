Delete your Hadithly account

Delete in the app

Open Hadithly and go to Settings, Account, Delete account. Review the warning and confirm deletion. This removes your sign-in identity and private Hadithly data, including bookmarks, favorites, notes, reading progress, notification tokens, proposals, and reports.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@hadithly.app from the address on your Hadithly account. Write “Hadithly account deletion” in the subject. We may ask you to verify control of the account before deletion.

What deletion does not do

Deleting your Hadithly account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase. Stores and RevenueCat may retain transaction records where required for fraud prevention, accounting, legal, or tax purposes.
