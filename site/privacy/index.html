Privacy policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Hadithly can be used without an account. Hadithly is operated by the developer identified as the seller on its store listing. This policy explains what we collect when you use Hadithly, why it is needed, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

If you create an account, we collect your email address, account identifier, optional name and username, and authentication records. Hadithly stores your chosen languages, notification preferences, private bookmarks, favorites, notes, reading position, translation proposals, and report reasons.

If you enable notifications, we store a push notification token and device platform. If you buy Hadithly Pro, we process your product identifier, purchase status, subscription expiry, and entitlement history. Apple or Google handles payment-card details. Hadithly does not receive them.

Our service providers may process device information, IP addresses, request metadata, and security logs needed to operate and protect their services.

How we use data

We use this data to authenticate your account, sync private reading data, deliver requested notifications, provide subscription access, enforce AI translation limits, review translation proposals, prevent abuse, answer support requests, and keep the service secure.

Hadithly has no ads. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-company tracking.

AI translations

When you request an AI translation, Hadithly sends the hadith text and target language to Google Gemini through the Hadithly backend. A community proposal can also include the proposed translation. These requests do not include your email address or profile name. AI text is labeled and is not presented as authoritative religious guidance.

Service providers

Hadithly uses Clerk for authentication, Convex for application data and backend functions, RevenueCat for subscription status, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, Google Gemini for requested AI translations, and Sunnah.now as a hadith content provider. Apple and Google also process purchases made through their stores. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and private app data while your account exists. You can delete the account in Hadithly under Settings, Account, Delete account. Deletion removes the Clerk identity and private Hadithly records. It anonymizes generated translation and audit references that must remain to preserve service integrity.

Account deletion does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. The stores and RevenueCat may retain transaction records for fraud prevention, accounting, legal, and tax obligations. Manage or cancel the subscription in your store account.

To request deletion without the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Security and international processing

Hadithly uses HTTPS in transit and limits production access to the services needed to operate the app. Providers may process data outside your country. Their contractual and legal safeguards apply to that processing.

Private usage statistics

Hadithly can share grouped outcomes to help improve onboarding, reading reliability, offline downloads, translations and purchases. This setting is separate from crash reporting and can be turned off in Settings, Privacy & data, Share private usage statistics. It does not collect reading text, searches, notes, saved items, evidence, hadith or collection identifiers, exact positions, languages, account identifiers, device identifiers, email, or notification tokens.

The apps calculate short flow outcomes locally and send bounded counter summaries through Convex. Summaries have a random batch identifier for retry deduplication only; it is never joined to an account or exported. Pending summaries expire within 24 hours. Receipt cleanup runs hourly, with bounded batches drained during the same cleanup cycle. Convex groups the counts by day and platform before sending them to PostHog's EU service. Small unpublished buckets expire after seven days and published aggregate counts in Convex expire after 90 days. PostHog's free service retains aggregate analytics for at least one year and may retain them longer; Hadithly does not promise a shorter vendor retention period.

Signing in does not identify you to analytics. Account deletion or opting out clears pending statistics and stops new collection on that device. A request already sent may complete. Published grouped totals have no account binding and cannot be located or removed as your individual history. Hadithly does not enable session replay, person profiles, advertising attribution, or analytics on this website.

Your choices

You can read without an account, decline notifications, restore or cancel store purchases, edit your account details, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@hadithly.app.

Changes

We will update the effective date and this page when the policy changes materially.

Optional error reports

Native crash reporting starts off. If you enable Send crash reports in Privacy & data, Hadithly sends redacted code stacks, build identifiers and coarse failure codes to Sentry. We remove exception messages, account identifiers, reading text, notes, evidence, queries, URLs, screenshots and breadcrumbs. Reports are not joined to your account. Sentry also receives your network address while delivering a report; provider privacy and security terms apply.

Turning reporting off stops new reports and clears this device's pending crash files. A request already in flight may complete. Already received events follow Sentry's 30-day event retention; uploaded build symbols are retained separately for crash diagnosis. Account deletion cannot locate anonymous reports by account identity. For a provider deletion request, contact support.

Backend diagnostics contain fixed failure codes and release/environment information, with no account or content data. Google Cloud Monitoring checks fixed public health URLs. These checks never generate translations or notifications.

This optional browser choice sends one fixed page-failure code and build information per page. It sends no page path, query, reading text or account data. It is separate from the native crash setting.
